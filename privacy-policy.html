Welcome to Elvish Army. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide while using our application and services.

Depending on how you use Elvish Army, we may process:

  • Username or display name
  • Community posts and comments

  • Likes, comments and replies
  • Device-generated identifiers used for app functionality

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the Elvish Army app

3. User-Generated Content

Elvish Army allows users to voluntarily share posts, comments, photos and videos through community features.

Content submitted to public community features may be visible to other users. Please do not upload sensitive personal information or content that you do not have permission to share.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us host, operate, maintain or secure the application. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect users, the service or other parties.

App data may be stored on servers used to operate the Elvish Army service. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce policies and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Elvish Army is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

8. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services for hosting, infrastructure, analytics or other technical functions. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices

You may choose what information and content you provide through the application. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through public community features.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided within the Elvish Army application or its official service channels.
